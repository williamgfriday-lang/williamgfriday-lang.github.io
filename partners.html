How it works

  1. 1

    You build or rebuild the site.

    Your work, your client, your roof — nothing about that changes.

  2. 2

    We verify it.

    A blind specialist walks the site's critical paths by hand with a screen reader — up to about 25 pages — and delivers the barrier map: what blocks a real user, where, and what fixing takes.

  3. 3

    Your client gets the record.

    A finished site plus evidence that a person checked it — the two things a machine scan alone can't give them.
