Accessibility statement

Accessibility is the work we sell — so our own house comes first. This page states what we commit to on braillebridges.com, how this site is built and checked, and what to do if it ever blocks you.

Our commitment

Braille Bridges LLC aims to conform this website to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. We say "aims" on purpose: accessibility is ongoing work, not a certificate, and we hold our own site to the same honest standard we sell — a real check, by a real person, that we can point to.

This site is tested by our founder, William Friday — a legally blind screen-reader user who navigates the web without sight every day. The same hands that would verify your site verify this one.

How this site is built

  • Semantic structure throughout: real headings, landmarks, and lists, so a screen reader can skim and navigate the page the way a sighted visitor does.
  • Full keyboard access, a skip-to-content link, and a visible focus outline on every link and control.
  • High-contrast black text on yellow, in large print — the same format standard we use in our own correspondence.
  • Text descriptions for images, and content that respects reduced-motion settings.
  • No street address is published here because Braille Bridges is a service-area business — Grand Rapids and Kent County, Michigan. Our door is the phone and the inbox below.

If this site blocks you

Tell us. A barrier on our own site is a barrier we want to know about — and fixing it is our job, not yours.

Call (616) 349-8187 or email williamgfriday@gmail.com. Tell us the page and what happened, and we will fix it and tell you when it's fixed.

If you'd rather not use this site at all, everything on it can be done by phone or email — the free first-page check, the request forms, all of it. The website is one door, never the only door.

This statement was published November 26, 2026. We review this site as it changes, and this statement with it. — William Friday, Founder, Braille Bridges LLC, Grand Rapids, Michigan.
